    How it works

    Every problem gets a ticket, an owner and a deadline

    CampusFix carries each campus problem through assignment, SLA tracking, live chat and reporter verification, until it is truly fixed.

    1. Open
    2. Triaged
    3. Assigned
    4. In Progress
    5. Resolved
    6. Verified
    7. Closed
    1. 1

      Step 01 · Report

      Report once. Get a trackable ticket.

      Pick the category, building, floor and room, set a priority and attach a photo. CampusFix creates a unique Ticket ID the moment you submit.

      Category & location Photo evidence Low → Critical

      Step 02 · Route & assign

      Routed to the right department, automatically.

      Category and location decide which department owns the problem. The manager then assigns a responsible staff member, or reassigns when needed.

      Step 03 · SLA & escalation

      The clock starts the moment it's assigned.

      Every category has a resolution time based on priority. If the SLA runs out, the ticket is marked as breached and escalates on its own.

      Step 04 · Work & communicate

      Talk directly to the person fixing it.

      Each ticket has its own live chat between the reporter and the assigned staff. Share photos, ask for details and follow the work, all saved as conversation history.

    5. Step 05 · Verify & close

      Closed only when you say it's fixed.

      Staff mark the ticket as Resolved and submit evidence. The reporter then verifies the result. If it isn't fixed, one tap reopens the ticket and the staff work on it again.
